What is Ahram Canadian University?

Al-Ahram Canadian University is a private university in 6th of October City, Egypt. It was established by Al-Ahram Egyptian daily newspaper. It includes a Faculty of Pharmacy, School of Business, Faculty of Computer and Information Technology, Faculty of Mass Communication, Faculty of Oral and Dental Medicine and Faculty of Engineering
